SACRED MOMENTS · TIMELESS TRADITIONS

A wedding journey blessed by tradition

From the first prayer to the seven sacred steps, every ritual carries a blessing for the life you begin together.

01

Temple & Mandapam

A sacred setting framed by banana trees, mango leaves, flowers and the glow of traditional lamps.

02

Gowri Pooja

The bride offers prayers to Goddess Gowri, seeking blessings for harmony, strength and a joyful married life.

03

Kanyadhanam

A deeply meaningful family blessing as the bride's parents entrust the couple's new journey to love and care.

04

Mangalyadharana

The sacred mangalyam is tied, marking a cherished promise of commitment, respect and lifelong companionship.

05

Talambralu

A joyful shower of turmeric-tinted rice, symbolising prosperity, abundance and happiness for the couple.

06

Sapta Adgulu · Saptapadi

Seven sacred steps that represent shared vows, support, prosperity, health and a lifetime of friendship.

07

Arundhati Nakshatram

A symbolic moment of looking toward Arundhati, celebrated as an emblem of devotion and enduring partnership.

08

Akshintalu & Blessings

Elders shower the newlyweds with blessings for a peaceful home, shared joy and a prosperous future.

Ritual names and order vary between Telugu families and communities. Please confirm the sequence with your family priest.
